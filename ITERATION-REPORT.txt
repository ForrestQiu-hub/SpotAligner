第一部分:开发历程 / Part 1: How it was built
====================================

按开发顺序讲述每一步做了什么、为什么那样决定。依据是代码自身的注释、参数表的演进与各阶段
留下的测试。
In the order of development: what each step did and why it was decided that way, taken from the
comments in the code, the evolution of the parameter list, and the tests each stage left behind.

第 1 步 · 距离对齐(插件最初的样子)
一个单声道对象、一个距离、一个声速:输出 = 输入延迟「距离 ÷ 声速」,让点话筒与主话筒在时间上
对齐。声速可手输,也可由温度、湿度、气压算出(湿度用 Cramer 近似 —— 它对声速影响很小,但对
20 kHz 附近的吸收很明显,写清楚比忽略它诚实)。参数表在此定型,顺序后来一直沿用,状态版本
兼容也靠它。
Step 1 · Distance alignment (what the plug-in first was). One mono object, one distance, one
speed of sound; the output is the input delayed by distance ÷ speed. The speed comes from air
conditions by Cramer's approximation. The parameter list took its shape here, and that ordering is
what later made the state compatible across every version.

第 2 步 · 从"延时器"变成"声像器"
加入点话筒角度与主话筒制式。核心认识:点话筒的左右分配不是一个音量旋钮,而是"这个声源在
主阵列里本来会是什么样"。于是 AB 以时间差为主、XY 以电平差为主、ORTF 两者兼有。为了让左右各自
不同,输入总线从单声道改成立体声,内部再把 L+R 相加成那一个声源对象。
Step 2 · From a delay to a panner. The spot angle and the main array technique arrived, and
with them the realisation that the distribution is not a volume knob but what the source would have
been in the array. The input bus became stereo so the two sides can differ.

第 3 步 · 几何与线索模型(纯头文件)
把物理从插件里抽出来放进 `spotPanner.h` / `formatRules.h` / `panoramaGeometry.h` / `soundSpeed.h`,
不依赖 VST 与界面 —— 这一步让后来所有东西都能脱离宿主被测试。模型包含:到每支话筒的精确
距离(含高度差)÷ 声速 = 该侧延时;指向性 `a + (1−a)·cos φ` 叠加距离衰减;等功率归一化;以及
线索相加 —— 电平差满偏 18 dB、时间差满偏 1.5 ms。
Step 3 · Geometry and cue model (pure headers). Four headers with no dependency on VST or the
GUI, which is what allowed everything to be tested without a host: distance over speed of sound,
directivity with the 1/r law, equal power normalisation, and the two cues with the classic
constants of equivalence stereophony.

第 4 步 · 制式表与最大拾音角(SRA)
一次关键的概念纠正:最大拾音角 ≠ 话筒指向角。前者是"这个阵列能把多宽的前方塞进像场",
由间距、张开角与指向性共同决定,而且有权威数据。据此建立六种制式的规则表,并得到时间差阵列的
解析式 `sin(SRA/2) = 声速 × 1.5 ms ÷ 间距` —— 它精确复现了表里的 AB 60/90/120 cm → 118/68/50°。
Step 4 · The technique table and the recording angle. The recording angle is not the microphone
angle. The table of six techniques was built from that, and the closed form for a time difference
array reproduces the published AB rows exactly.

第 5 步 · 像场位置与畸变
同一个线索模型回答:这个声源最后落在像场哪里(0 = 中间,±1 = 一只音箱)。两类阵列在此分道扬镳:
间距型在覆盖角之外饱和(时间差有上限),指向型继续扩张(电平差没有上限)。这就是"同样
宽度的 AB 与 ORTF 声像感觉不同"的数学表述。
Step 5 · Image position and its distortion. A spaced array saturates past its recording angle
because its time difference has a ceiling; an aimed array keeps expanding because its level
difference has none.

第 6 步 · 单声道/立体声轨道检测
轨道只有一条声道时插件做不了该做的事,于是:数清轨道真正给出的声道,不足两个就原样通过并
在面板说明;轨道一变成双声道立刻恢复处理,无需重载。(这一步引出的 bug 见 2.1–2.3。)
Step 6 · Mono and stereo track detection. Fewer than two channels means pass through and say so;
the moment the track becomes stereo the processing resumes with no reload.

第 7 步 · 德卡树真正的中置话筒
德卡树不是"一个很大的 AB"。它有中置话筒,而且真的参与 DSP:独立延时线,按三支话筒的几何
各算一条路径,等量送入左右。这解释了它为什么中置结实(中置在左右线前方,离声源更近),
以及它为什么会稀释左右线索 —— 同样的左右间距,加中置后覆盖角变宽(1.5 m + 1 m:40° → 87°)。
Step 7 · The Decca Tree centre microphone, for real. Its own delay line, fed to both sides,
which explains both the firm middle and the way it dilates the outer pair's cues.

第 8 步 · 读数架构的改写(一次重要转向)
视图一开始自己去读控件数值,这条路先后失败两次(2.2、2.3)。最终改成:**控制器用与音频完全相同
的纯函数算出结果 → 发布成只读参数 → 宿主转发 → 标签显示文本 → 视图只解析文本**。从此显示与
音频不可能给出两个不同答案。
Step 8 · Rewriting how readouts reach the panel. Views stopped reading controls; the controller
computes with the same pure functions the audio uses and publishes read only parameters; the views
parse label text.

第 9 步 · 强度差:大间距也要算
你指出 AB 在大间距、近距离时不只有时间差。对:两支话筒到声源距离不同,1/r 定律在 3 m 间距 /
4 m 距离时给出超过 4 dB,而 20 m 外不到 1 dB。时间差方向相反 —— 随距离略微增大并逼近
「间距 × sin 角度」。两条互补规律都进了模型,都有断言。
Step 9 · The intensity difference at wide spacings. More than 4 dB at 3 m of spacing and 4 m of
distance, under 1 dB at 20 m, while the time difference grows slightly with distance towards
spacing × sin of the angle.

第 10 步 · 制式从"紧身衣"变成"预设"
原先三项都被制式名锁死,标准 AB 之外的阵列无法表达。改成:制式 = 预设,选定后三项都可改;
每个制式只提供它真正会用的选项(重合 XY 固定角度、只有心形与八字;ORTF/NOS/DIN 三档心形家族;
AB 与德卡树为全向,别家不许选全向)。改动后标出 `edited`,改回标准值标记自动消失。
Step 10 · A technique became a preset. All three values stay editable, each technique offers
only what it is done with, and a changed geometry is marked `edited` until the values are its own
again.

第 11 步 · 界面
黑金配色、可拖动的扇形图(点与角度是同一个值的两种看法)、读数区、格式面板;窗口高度从 420
压到 336。界面自身的错误见 2.10–2.12。
Step 11 · The panel. The palette, the draggable dome display, the readouts, the format panel,
and the window brought down from 420 to 336.

第 12 步 · 验证设施
无头测试(纯头文件脱离宿主独立编译,三套)、契约校验(标签与参数枚举、各视图子控件形状)、
布局检查(越界、重叠、父容器层级)、Steinberg 验证器 47 项、状态版本链 v1→v8。最强的一次验证
是 2.8:模型不知道 Blumlein 是什么,却从"重合 + ±45° + 八字"算出 75.7°,与表里的 76° 差 0.3°。
Step 12 · Verification. Headless suites, a contract check, a layout check with parent
containment, the validator's 47 tests, and a state chain from v1 to v8. The strongest check is 2.8.

第 13 步 · 分发
一键安装脚本(自动判断权限)、单文件 zip、五个语言版本的 README(英/中/德/法/日,全部带 UTF-8
BOM 以免乱码)、厂商名改为 Forrest's Plug-ins。本机开发安装用 junction 指向构建目录,每次重新
构建后立刻是最新版。
Step 13 · Distribution. An install script that picks the right folder for the rights it has, a
single zip, a README in five languages with a BOM, and the vendor renamed. The development install
is a junction to the build folder, so every rebuild is immediately current.

现在的功能 / What it does now
------------------------

- 距离对齐:声源距离 ÷ 声速得到延时,声速可手输,也可由温度、湿度、气压计算。
- 制式驱动的分声道处理:主话筒制式(AB / ORTF / XY / NOS / DIN / Decca Tree)决定左右
  各自的延时与电平,而不是一个普通声像旋钮。
- 单声道轨道检测:轨道给不出两个声道时,对象原样通过,面板给出警告。
- 覆盖角与几何标记:面板显示阵列覆盖角、左右延时与电平;阵列被改离该制式标准几何时标出 edited。
- 德卡树中置话筒:真的参与 DSP(独立延时线,等量送入左右),不是"当成一个大 AB"。
- 分发:五个语言版本的 README、一键安装脚本、单文件 zip。

- Distance alignment: distance divided by the speed of sound, which can be entered by hand or
  computed from temperature, humidity and air pressure.
- Format driven per channel processing: the main array (AB / ORTF / XY / NOS / DIN / Decca Tree)
  decides the delay and the level of each side, instead of a plain level panner.
- Mono track detection: when the track cannot carry two channels the object passes through and
  the panel says so.
- Coverage and geometry marker: the panel shows the recording angle and both delays and levels,
  and marks the array as edited when it no longer matches the geometry of its technique.
- Decca Tree centre microphone: it really is in the audio path (its own delay line, fed to both
  sides), not treated as a wide AB.
- Distribution: README in five languages, a one click install script, a single zip.

修正记录 / Corrections
------------------

早期修正 / Early corrections

E1 · 黑金配色与控件标签表。两个开局错误:颜色写成 `#AARRGGBB`(VSTGUI 要 `#RRGGBBAA`),
以及漏了 `<control-tags>` 表 —— 于是控件与参数对不上,界面一片空白。
E1 · Palette and tag table. Colours written the wrong way round and a missing tag table, so
nothing was bound at all.

E2 · 自定义视图改了状态却不重绘。`CView::setVisible()` 不触发重绘,而在 `drawRect` 内部
请求重绘会被丢弃。修法:所有自定义视图只在 `onIdle()` 里改状态,并同时 `invalid()` 自己与整个
frame。
E2 · State changed without a repaint. `setVisible()` does not repaint, and a repaint asked for
from inside a paint is dropped. Every custom view applies state in `onIdle()` now.

E3 · 延时读数不跟随高度。重算读数的函数里有一份手写的参数清单,漏了两个高度参数。修法:
改成"除读数自身以外一律重算",从此不必维护清单。
E3 · The delay readout ignored the heights. A hand written list of triggers had missed them;
everything is recalculated now except the readouts themselves.

E4 · 零散的编译与构建问题。`str16BufferSize` 不能加命名空间限定;一个辅助函数在定义之前被
调用;一次编辑残留导致大括号失衡;`LINK1104` 只表示宿主还占着那个 DLL,关掉即可。
E4 · Small build ones. A namespace qualified macro, a helper called before it was defined, a
leftover brace, and `LINK1104`, which only means a host still holds the DLL.

中期与近期 / Middle and recent

2.1 单声道轨道检测永远报 0
现象:立体声轨道上显示 "0 channels",面板一直警告。
根因:处理器把当前轨道声道数与自己的默认值比较,相同就不上报;而控制器一侧由宿主
初始化为 0,于是编辑器永远停在 0。
修法:改为与自己上一次真正上报的值比较(初值设为 −1),第一块必然上报。
Verification:立体声轨道立即显示 "Track output: 2 channels"。

Mono detection always reported zero.
Symptom: a stereo track showed "0 channels" and kept warning.
Cause: the processor compared the track channel count with its own default and stayed silent when
they matched, while the controller side had been initialised to zero by the host.
Fix: compare with what was last reported (initialised to −1), so the first block always reports.
Verified: a stereo track immediately shows "Track output: 2 channels".

2.2 文本标签收不到数值
现象:从控件读取数值恒为 0。
根因:VST3 编辑器对 `CTextLabel` 只调用 `setText()`,从不给它数值
(见 `vst3editor.cpp` 的 `updateControlValue`)。
修法:改为读取标签文本并解析出数字(这条路径后来成为读数的标准做法)。

A text label never receives a numeric value.
Symptom: reading a value from that control always gave zero.
Cause: for a `CTextLabel` the VST3 editor only calls `setText()`, never sets a value.
Fix: read the label's text and parse the number out of it — a path that later became the standard
way every readout is delivered.

2.3 控件嵌套时读值恒为 0(架构性教训)
现象:间距、温度、湿度、气压四个字段读出来全是各参数的最小值(日志证据:
`spacing=10 temp=-20 hum=1 pres=80`),而界面上显示的值完全正确。
根因:凡是嵌在普通容器里的控件,通过视图读它的值都得到 0;直接子控件则正常。
修法:不再让视图读控件。控制器用共享的纯函数算出结果,写成只读参数,宿主转发给
标签,视图只解析文本。显示与音频从此不可能算出两个不同答案。

Reading a nested control always gives zero (the architectural lesson).
Symptom: spacing, temperature, humidity and pressure all read as their minimum values while the
panel displayed them correctly.
Cause: any control nested inside a plain container answered zero; direct children answered fine.
Fix: views no longer read controls at all. The controller computes the values with the same pure
functions the audio path uses, publishes them as read only parameters, and the views parse the
label text. Display and audio can no longer disagree.

2.4 输入 55 度显示成 0.6
现象:`Aimed at` 输入 55,显示 0.6。
根因:`RangeParameter::toString` 收到的是归一化值,55 ÷ 90 正好是 0.61。
修法:委托基类做归一化到物理值的转换,再追加单位(与已正确的 `ParamRef` 一致)。

55 degrees displayed as 0.6.
Cause: `RangeParameter::toString` receives the normalised value, and 55/90 is 0.61.
Fix: let the base class convert to the plain value, then append the unit.

2.5 列表选"心形"却显示 Figure-8
现象:选 ORTF 后指向性显示成 Figure-8。
根因:VST3 列表参数的归一化是 `序号 ÷ (项数 − 1)`,我用了 `÷ 项数`,整体错了一位。
修法:统一约定,并在建立列表时显式设置 `stepCount = 项数 − 1`。

A list entry landed one row off.
Cause: a VST3 list parameter normalises as `index / (count − 1)`; the code used `index / count`.
Fix: one convention everywhere, with `stepCount` set explicitly when the list is built.

2.6 NOS 默认变 20 cm、Decca Tree 数值越界、edited 永不出现
现象:选 NOS 间距变成 20 cm(那是 DIN 的);选 Decca Tree 出现越界读数;改动几何后
`edited` 不出现。
根因:`parameters.h` 里有一个陈旧重复常量 `kMainFormatCount = 7`(早期含 MS 时的值),
它遮蔽了 `formatRules.h` 里正确的 6,制式范围因此变成 `{0, 6}`,所有制式整体偏移一位。
修法:删除陈旧定义,改为显式限定 `Panorama::kMainFormatCount`,并在代码里写明原因;
同时检查其余计数量,确认没有第二处重复。

NOS defaulted to 20 cm, Decca Tree went out of range, edited never appeared.
Cause: one stale duplicate constant in `parameters.h` (`kMainFormatCount = 7`, from when MS was in
the list) shadowed the correct value in `formatRules.h`, so the range became `{0, 6}` and every
format was shifted by one entry.
Fix: the stale copy is gone, the use is qualified, and the reason is written down. All other counts
were checked for the same mistake.

2.7 改一下间距,SRA 从 96 跳到 133
现象:标准 ORTF 显示表里的 96 度,轻轻改一下间距就跳到 133 度。
根因:模型对"电平 + 时间"混合制式的标定与权威表不同(表里 ORTF 是 96)。
修法:用该制式自己的标准阵列算"满偏阈值",混合制式因此在表值附近连续变化。
(第一版我把阈值取自被问的那个阵列,成了循环定义 —— 自己发现并改正。)
验证:15 / 17 / 25 / 40 / 69 cm → 100 / 96 / 82 / 64 / 44 度,平滑无跳变。

The recording angle jumped from 96 to 133 when the spacing was touched.
Cause: the model's calibration for mixed level-and-time arrays differed from the published table.
Fix: the threshold is taken from the technique's own standard array, so a changed geometry moves
smoothly away from the published value. (The first attempt took it from the array being asked
about, which is circular; that was caught and corrected.)
Verified: 15 / 17 / 25 / 40 / 69 cm give 100 / 96 / 82 / 64 / 44 degrees.

2.8 XY + 八字算出 180 度,而表里是 76 度
现象:Blumlein(重合的 ±45 度八字对)模型给出 180 度,权威表是 76 度。
根因:重合阵列在 90 度时两个增益符号相反、大小相等,`(gL + gR)` 为零,
"外话筒占比"成了 `0/0 = NaN`;NaN 让二分比较恒假,解算器于是认为"永远达不到",返回整个前方。
修法:分母取幅度、趋零则份额为零;并明确"两声道极性相反 = 已到极值",不是"没有差异"。
验证:现在得 75.7 度,与表里的 76 度相差 0.3 度 —— 模型事先不知道 Blumlein,
是从"重合 + ±45 度 + 八字"三个事实独立算出来的。

A Blumlein pair came out as 180 degrees where the table says 76.
Cause: for a coincident array at ninety degrees the two gains are equal and opposite, so the
"share carried by the outer pair" was 0/0 = NaN; a NaN made every comparison false and the solver
reported the whole front.
Fix: the share is a magnitude over a magnitude, a vanishing denominator leaves no share, and two
sides of opposite polarity count as the far end of the image rather than as no difference.
Verified: 75.7 degrees, within 0.3 of the published 76 — derived from coincident, ±45° and figure
of eight alone, without being told what a Blumlein pair is.

2.9 超心形与八字的响应为负
现象:指向性改为超心形或八字后,覆盖角读数失真。
根因:离轴足够远时响应为负(反相),被当成"差值很小",破坏了求解所需的单调性。
修法:按"极性相反 = 已越过极值"处理。
验证:同一 ORTF 几何下 心形 96 / 超心形 75.5 / 八字 41.6 度,顺序合理。

Negative off axis response.
Cause: past its null a directional microphone answers with the opposite sign; treating that as a
small difference broke the monotonicity the solver needs.
Fix: opposite polarity counts as past the extreme.
Verified: the same ORTF geometry gives 96 (cardioid), 75.5 (supercardioid), 41.6 (figure of eight).

2.10 点话筒的 Angle 行凭空消失
现象:面板上 SPOT MICROPHONE 一整段不见了。
根因:我删掉一行时把格式面板高度改小了,里面那些行的坐标没动,于是最后一整段落在
面板之外被裁掉(VSTGUI 按父容器裁剪子控件)。
修法:面板高度回到能容纳最后一行;并给布局检查增加父容器层级检查,这类问题以后
会被直接报出来。

The spot microphone angle silently disappeared.
Cause: the panel was made shorter while the rows inside kept their coordinates, so the last block
fell outside it and was clipped.
Fix: the panel is tall enough again, and the layout check now compares every child with its own
container, not only with the window.

2.11 面板中间出现空洞
现象:切到重合制式时,面板中间多出一块空白。
根因:会被隐藏的行(间距、中置)排在中间。
修法:把条件行移到最后,隐藏时留下的空白落在面板底部。

A hole in the middle of the panel.
Cause: the rows that can be hidden sat in the middle of the panel.
Fix: they are last now, so hiding one leaves the space at the bottom.

2.12 关掉视窗再打开,扇形图的动点回到原点
现象:关闭视窗(不是移除插件)再打开,动点回到扇形图原点,但参数数值完全没变。
根因:视图缓存了控件指针;重开视窗时编辑器会把整套控件重建,缓存的旧对象一直回答
它创建时的初始值 0,于是点被摆到中心。
修法:每次 idle 重新按 tag 查找控件,绝不缓存;拖动时不被参数反拉。
验证:重开视窗后动点停在原处。

The point in the display fell back to the centre after closing and reopening the window.
Cause: the view cached the control pointers; reopening the window rebuilds every control, and the
cached objects kept answering with the value they were created with.
Fix: the controls are looked up again on every idle and never cached; while dragging, the point is
not pulled back by the parameters.
Verified: the point stays where it was.

刻意没有做 / Deliberately not done
-----------------------------

- 点话筒指向性不进 DSP。那支话筒的指向性已经录在它的信号里了,再套一遍是重复计算
  (双份近讲、双份离轴衰减)。它现在的用途是一句提示,说明该制式下对齐值不值得、梳状滤波风险多大。
- AAX(Pro Tools)。SDK 里虽然自带 VST3→AAX 包装器,但 Pro Tools 拒绝加载未签名的 AAX,
  需要 Avid 审批 + PACE 签名 + 实体 iLok;或者用包装宿主(如免费的 Modalics Plugin Buddy)直接跑 VST3。
- AU / macOS。SDK 里也自带 AUv2/AUv3 包装器和 CMake 助手,且不需要任何签名,
  唯一门槛是一台 Mac;Logic 与 GarageBand 只认 AU。
- 实时电平表(曾按需求实现后完整删除)、侧链自动定位(留待 2.0)。

- The spot microphone's polar pattern is not applied to the audio. That microphone's pattern is
  already in its recording, so applying it again would count it twice. It is used as a note instead.
- AAX (Pro Tools). The SDK does ship a VST3 to AAX wrapper, but Pro Tools refuses unsigned AAX:
  it needs Avid approval, PACE signing and a physical iLok — or a wrapper host that itself is signed.
- AU / macOS. The SDK also ships AUv2 and AUv3 wrappers and a CMake helper, with no signing gate
  at all; the only requirement is a Mac. Logic and GarageBand require AU.
- Live level meters (implemented, then removed on request) and side chain auto positioning
  (kept for 2.0).

已知风险 / Known risks
------------------

- 已根治:四个自定义视图(含 `PanoramaView`)过去都是"找到即缓存"的写法,
  重开视窗时缓存的控件已被销毁,于是出现 2.12 那类幽灵问题。现在每次刷新都重新按 tag 查找,
  任何控件重建都不会再让视图失效。代价是每帧几百次指针比较,可忽略。
- 两个电平表参数保留但不再使用,目的是不回退参数编号、旧工程仍可读。
- "距离强度差(1/r)" 与 "18 dB / 1.5 ms" 在混合制式下的组合仍是近似;端点与权威表一致
  (时间型 AB、电平型 XY/Blumlein 都是精确的)。

- Fixed for good: all four custom views (including `PanoramaView`) used to cache what they found.
  Reopening the window rebuilds every control, which is what produced the ghost of 2.12. Every lookup now
  happens again on each refresh, at the cost of a few hundred pointer comparisons per frame.
- Two meter parameters are kept but unused, so that parameter numbering — and older projects — stay valid.
- The combination of the 1/r intensity difference with the 18 dB / 1.5 ms rule remains an
  approximation for mixed arrays; the end points match the published table exactly.

2026-10-16 · 1.0.0 · 修订二(扇形图重绘,根治)
  · 现象:输入距离或角度后,扇形图的点不动;关掉视窗再打开,点停在原点。
  · 根因:扇形图只调用 invalid() 标记【自己】,从不调用 frame->invalid()。
    frame 是把子视图画进自己的缓存合成里的,只标脏自己的视图可以不重绘 ——
    于是内部成员一直跟着参数在变,画面却停在第一次绘制时的样子;而第一次绘制发生在
    读到参数之前,所以重开后看到的正是原点。
    同一个编辑器里另外三个自定义视图都同时 invalid() 自己与 frame,它们一直正常 ——
    这条规律在报告里早写着,只有扇形图漏了。
  · 修法:抽出 refresh()(invalid() + frame->invalid()),onIdle 与拖动都走它;
    并去掉绘制结束时多余的 setDirty(false)(另外三个视图都没有这一步)。
  · 上一轮加的方位线与距离圆环保留 —— 它们让点在贴近圆心时仍可读。
2026-10-16 · 1.0.0 · Revision 2 (the display's repaint, root cause)
  · Symptom: typing a distance or an angle did not move the point; reopening the window left it
    at the centre.
  · Cause: the display called invalid() on itself and never frame->invalid(). A frame draws its
    children into a cached composition of its own, so a view that marks only itself dirty can go
    on showing what it drew last: the members followed the parameters all along while the picture
    stayed as the first paint had left it - and that first paint happened before the parameters
    were read, which is why reopening showed the centre. The other three custom views of this
    editor invalidate the frame as well and have always repainted correctly; the rule was already
    written down in this report, and this one view had missed it.
  · Fix: one refresh() (invalid() plus frame->invalid()), used by both the idle callback and
    dragging; the redundant setDirty(false) at the end of the paint is gone.
  · The bearing line and the distance ring from the previous round are kept: they make the point
    readable while it sits near the centre.
2026-10-16 · 1.0.0 · 修订三(扇形图:重开后仍不动)
  · 现象:操作后点能跟上参数了(上一版的重绘修复生效),但【关掉视窗再打开】后点仍在圆心,
    方位线也停在 0 度 —— 尽管角度/距离输入框里是正确的值。
  · 根因:重开视窗的【第一次绘制】发生在 idle 回调运行之前。绘制用的是构造时的默认位置,
    而当时还没有人读过参数;等到 idle 跑起来时,画面已经画完了,只有等下一次重绘才会更正 ——
    而那时除非用户再动一下,不会有下一次重绘。
  · 修法:在【每次绘制之前】先读一次参数(followParameters)。绘制期间不请求重绘,
    只是让这一帧的值本来就是对的 —— 于是重开后的第一帧就是正确位置,不依赖 idle 的时序。
  · 如实记录:这一个 bug 我改了三轮才对。前两轮(缓存指针、可读性)都是真问题,但不是主诉;
    真正该做的是【第一时间把扇形图与三个正常视图逐行比对】—— 一比就看出它缺 frame->invalid(),
    以及它把读取放在 idle 而不是绘制前。教训:同类组件要互相参照,而不是各自推理。
2026-10-16 · 1.0.0 · Revision 3 (the display, still stuck after reopening)
  · Symptom: the point followed the parameters once they were touched (the repaint fix worked), but
    after closing and reopening the window it sat at the centre with the bearing line at zero, while
    the angle and distance fields held the right values.
  · Cause: the first paint after a window is reopened happens before the idle callback has run. That
    paint used the position the view was constructed with, and nothing asked for another one.
  · Fix: read the parameters once more immediately before every paint. No repaint is requested
    during a paint; the frame simply already carries the right value, so the first frame after a
    reopen is correct and nothing depends on the order in which idle happens to run.
  · Honestly recorded: this took three rounds. The first two (a cached pointer, then readability)
    were real defects but not the complaint. The thing to do first was to compare this view line by
    line with the three views that work - which shows at once that it lacked frame->invalid() and
    that it read the parameters in idle instead of before painting.
